Privacy Policy

RunSync for iPhone. Effective November 19, 2026.

Summary

RunSync does not collect, store or share any of your data. The developer has no server and cannot see your workouts.

Health data

With your permission, RunSync reads workouts, workout routes, heart rate, distance, calories, steps and running metrics from Apple Health. It only reads; it never writes to or changes your Health data. It turns this data into files (CSV, JSON, GPX) that are saved on your iPhone and in your own iCloud Drive. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.

Where your data goes

  • Your iPhone and your iCloud Drive. Stored under your Apple Account. The developer has no access.
  • Email (optional). If you send the CSV by email, it goes through your own Mail account to the address you choose.
  • Google Sheets (optional). If you connect Google Sheets, RunSync signs in to your Google account on your iPhone and adds your workouts to a spreadsheet it creates in your own Google Drive. It requests only the drive.file permission, so it can see only that one file, not the rest of your Drive. The sign-in token is kept in your iPhone's Keychain. You can disconnect in RunSync › Settings or at myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party connections.

No tracking

RunSync has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no account. Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash and usage statistics if you have chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings.

Children

RunSync is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact
